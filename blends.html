What moves a price — stated plainly

Grind: no charge

Whole bean, drip, espresso, or French press grind — same price, chosen at checkout.

Bag size: +$14

A 2 lb bag adds $14 to any blend instead of buying two 12 oz bags — the better-value option is labeled as such.

Shipping: $4, free over $38

Flat $4 under $38, free at $38 and up — the threshold is printed here, not revealed at checkout.
