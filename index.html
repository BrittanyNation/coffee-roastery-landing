Roast dates, not mystery

The roast day is printed on the bag and repeated on the product page — freshness is a date, not a slogan.

Taste in plain words

Three tasting notes per blend, written for people who just want a good cup — no scoring systems, no jargon wall.

Subscription without traps

Pause, swap blends, or cancel from the subscription page itself. The exit is as visible as the sign-up.

Design notes

One page, one decision

The landing page has a single job — help a visitor pick a first bag or a simple subscription — so blends, trust facts, and the subscription path all live in one scroll.

Warmth with structure

Espresso carries the header, stats, and footer; ember marks only the actions and prices that matter; caramel warms the key numbers — badges always pair color with words.

Honest by default

Prices, roast schedule, and subscription terms appear before any form does. Nothing important waits until checkout.
